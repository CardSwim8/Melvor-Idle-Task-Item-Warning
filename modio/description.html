Task Keeper marks the items your Township tasks still need, so you don't sell them or burn them in a skill by accident. It also marks the ingredients that go into them, all the way down the crafting chain.

What it marks

  • Needed (amber disc): asked for by an incomplete main task or an active casual task.
  • Ingredient (violet diamond): goes into making something a task needs, through any recipe, shop purchase or item upgrade.
  • Possible (sky ring): something the casual task pool could ask for. Can be turned off.

Marked icons get a small corner badge whose shape and color give the status, in the bank and in skill menus.

See why

  • Hover or tap a marked item to see which tasks want it and the crafting chain that leads there.
  • In the bank, the selected item's sidebar has a Tasks tab with the same detail, how many to keep, and links that open each task in Township.
  • Selling several items at once? The confirmation lists any of them that your tasks need.

Settings

  • Show or hide items the casual task pool could ask for.
  • A colorblind-friendly palette.
  • Whether to mark icons in the bank, and in skill menus.

Good to know

  • No gameplay changes: it only marks icons and adds information. Nothing is locked, moved or sold for you.
  • Marks update as you complete tasks and as casual tasks come and go.
